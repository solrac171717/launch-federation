The Best Product Hunt Alternatives in 2026

Product Hunt still works — but it's crowded, the algorithm rewards hype more than substance, and one bad launch day means you wait months to try again. Here's where else indie founders are actually launching this year, and what each one is good for.

Why look beyond Product Hunt at all

Product Hunt's top-5-of-the-day format means most launches get buried by lunchtime. You get one shot, the backlink is nofollow, and success depends heavily on how big your existing audience already is before you launch — which is backwards for most early-stage founders. The directories below either spread launches out, guarantee a dofollow link, or both.

1. Launch Federation

A weekly directory where launches run Monday-to-Monday instead of competing in a single 24-hour sprint. Free listings get a real dofollow backlink as long as you keep the verification badge installed on your site — we check automatically every week, so it's not a one-time trick. Premium listings skip the weekly free-slot cap entirely and can pick any week. If you want sustained traffic and a backlink that still counts a year later instead of a single day of spikes, this is what we built it for. Submit your launch.

2. BetaList

One of the oldest startup-discovery lists, focused on pre-launch and early-stage products. Good for building a waitlist before you're fully live; less useful once your product already has paying customers.

3. Uneed

A fast-growing, founder-run directory with daily launches and an active community of builders upvoting each other. Worth submitting to alongside a handful of others rather than as your only launch.

4. Fazier

Lower competition than Product Hunt, with a simple free tier and paid options for guaranteed placement. A reasonable second or third stop in a multi-directory launch week.

5. Twelve Tools

A directory specifically for small, scrappy tools rather than funded startups — a good fit if what you built is closer to a utility than a full SaaS platform.

6. Dofollow.Tools

Does exactly what the name says: a directory built around giving builders a real dofollow backlink, which makes it one of the more SEO-minded options on this list.

7. KittyLaunch

A newer launch platform with a clean, simple submission flow and its own badge system, similar in spirit to how we handle verification here.

8. Huzzler

A community-driven product directory where other builders can discover, use, and give feedback on your tool — useful for early user interviews as much as traffic.

How to actually use this list

Don't pick one — submit to four or five of these in the same week, including at least one that gives you a genuine dofollow backlink (not just a badge for show). Stagger the ones with daily competition, and use a weekly one like Launch Federation as the anchor since it doesn't force you to compete for a single day's spotlight.
