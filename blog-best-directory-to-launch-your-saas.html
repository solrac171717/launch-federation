Best Directory to Launch Your SaaS (and How to Pick One)

"Submit your SaaS to 50 directories" is bad advice dressed up as a growth hack. Most of those links are nofollow, buried on pages Google barely crawls, or gone the moment your listing scrolls off the front page. Here's what actually separates a useful launch directory from a waste of an afternoon.

1. Does the backlink actually count?

Check whether the link back to your site is dofollow or nofollow — most directories default to nofollow, which does nothing for your search rankings. On Launch Federation, free listings get a dofollow link as long as the verification badge stays installed on your site; we recheck it weekly, so it's not something you can fake once and forget. If a directory never mentions dofollow at all, assume it's nofollow.

2. Is the listing permanent, or does it disappear?

A lot of "launch" sites bury your listing once the day ends, which means the backlink effectively dies with it. Look for directories where listings stay live permanently with their own URL — that's the difference between a one-day traffic spike and a link that keeps compounding in search results for years.

3. Can you actually win, or is it pay-to-win?

Some directories only surface listings that paid for placement, so a free submission is effectively invisible. Others, like ours, cap free slots at a fixed number per week (so there's real competition and real upvotes) while letting premium listings skip the cap and pick any week — free founders aren't competing against an unlimited queue of paid ones for the same spots.

4. Is there a real audience behind it, or just a list?

A directory with voting, comments, and a leaderboard gives you actual feedback and discovery, not just a static entry in a long list nobody browses. Favor directories where people show up to look at what's new that week.

5. What does it cost to skip the queue?

If you need to launch on a specific date — a product update, a fundraising announcement, a seasonal push — check whether the directory has a paid option that bypasses the wait. On Launch Federation, a $7 premium listing bypasses the free-slot cap and lets you choose any week, with no badge requirement.

Where to start

Don't rely on a single directory. Use one weekly, permanent-listing directory with a real dofollow link as your anchor — that's what Launch Federation is built for — and layer in two or three daily-launch sites for the initial traffic spike.
