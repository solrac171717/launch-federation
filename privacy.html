Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains what data Launch Federation collects and how it's used.

1. What we collect

When you sign in, we collect your email address (and, if you use Google, your Google profile email) via our authentication provider, Supabase. When you submit a listing, we store what you enter: product name, tagline, description, URL, logo, tags, and target market. When you buy a premium listing or ad slot, payment is handled directly by Stripe — we receive confirmation of payment, not your card number.

2. How we use it

To run your account, display your listing, show your ad if you've booked a slot, verify your badge weekly, and email you about your listing or account when needed. We don't sell your data.

3. Badge verification

To verify a free listing, our server fetches the public HTML of the URL you submitted and checks for our badge image. We don't store the page content, only the pass/fail result and the time it was checked.

4. Third parties we rely on

Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
Stripe — payment processing for premium listings and ad slots.
Vercel — website and serverless function hosting.
Google — optional sign-in via Google OAuth.

5. Your choices

You can request deletion of your account and listings at any time. Canceling an ad slot subscription stops future billing immediately.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out via the contact details on our homepage.

See also our Terms of Service.
