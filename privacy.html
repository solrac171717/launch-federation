Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ATLAS is a workspace where you keep track of the things you own. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and what you can ask us to do. Questions go to adftblyt@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

ATLAS (“we”, “us”) operates this website and the ATLAS app. For any privacy question or request, write to adftblyt@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

If you join the early-access list

  • Your email address (required).
  • Optional details you choose to give: first name, size of your portfolio, the types of assets you own, and what kind of investor you are.
  • The time you agreed and the exact consent text you accepted, so we can show that you agreed.

If you create an account and use the app

  • Your email address, used to sign you in with a link (there are no passwords).
  • The assets you enter: names, type, location, estimated value, purchase cost, yearly income and expenses, status, purchase date, notes, and the history of value changes. This can be sensitive financial information, so we treat it that way.
  • Technical data needed to keep you signed in, such as a session token stored in your browser.

Automatically, when you visit

Our hosting provider records standard server logs (for example IP address, browser, pages requested, and time) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign you in, save your assets, and show them back to you.
  • To contact you about ATLAS early access and launch updates, if you joined the list.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, we do not show advertising, and we do not use your assets to profile you or to train advertising systems.

4. Who else handles your data

We use a small number of providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Your account and assets are stored here.
  • Vercel: hosting of the website and its server functions.
  • Google Fonts: the Inter typeface is loaded from Google, which means your browser contacts Google servers (and shares your IP address) when a page loads.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own. We share information beyond them only if the law requires it.

5. Cookies and similar storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app stores a sign-in session in your browser, and, when it runs without an account, your assets in your browser’s local storage. This is needed for the app to work and stays on your device.

The website has hooks for privacy-friendly usage analytics, but they stay switched off unless you consent, and none is active today. If that changes we will update this page first.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your assets and account stay until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
  • Early-access details stay until you ask us to remove them or the early-access program ends.
  • Server logs are kept for a short period by our hosting provider.

7. Your choices and rights

You can delete any asset in the app at any time, and you can download your assets as a CSV file. To get a copy of your information, correct it, delete your account, or remove your email from the list, write to adftblyt@gmail.com from the email address on the account. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, to move your data elsewhere, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We honor these requests where the law applies.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Each account can only read and change its own assets, and this is enforced in the database itself. No online service can promise perfect security, so please protect your email account well, because anyone who can open your sign-in email can open your workspace.

9. Children

ATLAS is not meant for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and, when the change affects how we use your data, tell you in the app or by email.
