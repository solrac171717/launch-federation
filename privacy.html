Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Owned Map finds people who are asking for what you sell, scores how ready they are to buy, and shows them on a map. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect (about you and about the people who appear in your leads), why, who helps us run the service, and what you can ask us to do. Questions go to support@ownedmap.com.

1. Who we are

Owned Map (“we”, “us”) operates this website and the Owned Map app. For any privacy question or request, write to support@ownedmap.com.

2. What we collect

If you create an account and use the app

  • Your email address and the password you choose (we store only a protected form of the password).
  • Your business description: the website address you give, what we understood from that page (what you sell, to whom, the pain it removes, your tone, the phrases buyers use) and any edits you make to it.
  • Your leads and what you do with them: the status you set, the history of those changes, the reply drafts (and your edits to them) and the labels and settings of any read-only share links you create (a hash of the link secret, never the secret itself).
  • If you use the browser extension: a pairing code you make in the app (we store only a hash of it) the posts and profile locations the extension sends from the LinkedIn pages you open, and a note when an invitation or message you queued has been sent (the link to the profile). The messages you queue stay in your browser’s extension storage until sent. We never receive your LinkedIn password, cookies or session.
  • Technical data needed to keep you signed in, such as a session token stored in your browser.

About the people who appear in your leads

A lead is built from something a person published in public: the text of a post or comment, the public name or username of its author, a link to it, and, when it is stated in the post or on the author’s own public profile, their role, company and city. We add a score, the intent we read in it, and (for you) a drafted reply. We do not collect private messages, we do not log in to anyone’s account, and we do not buy contact lists. We do not look up email addresses or phone numbers. If you are one of these people and want your information removed, write to support@ownedmap.com with the link to your post and we will delete it from every account.

Automatically, when you visit

Our hosting provider records standard server logs (for example IP address, browser, pages requested, and time) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign you in, read your website, search for people asking for what you sell, score and place them, draft replies, save everything and show it back to you.
  • To show your leads, in read-only form, to the people you give a share link to. Anyone who has the link can see the leads (and your reply drafts, only if you chose to include them) until it expires or you turn it off.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We never post, send or click anything on your behalf, on any site. We do not sell your information, we do not show advertising, and we do not use your data to train advertising systems.

4. Who else handles your data

We use a small number of providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Your account, business description, leads and share links are stored here.
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions, only if you start a plan. Stripe receives your email and the payment details you type on its own page; we receive the status of your plan and its size, never your card number. If you start a free trial from our website, we create your account from the email you typed on Stripe’s page, and you then choose your own password.
  • OpenAI: when you describe your business, the text of your public web page (or what you typed) is sent to OpenAI so it can work out what you sell. When we score leads, the text of the public posts we found, and the author’s public profile text when there is one, is sent to OpenAI to be scored and to draft a reply. Under OpenAI’s API terms this data is not used to train their models.
  • Hacker News (Y Combinator) and Algolia: our server searches Hacker News posts through its public search service with the phrases we derived from your business, and reads the public profile text of authors it found. Your business is not named in those requests.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): when a lead’s post states a place we do not know, that place name (for example a city) is sent to this public map service to find its coordinates.
  • Vercel: hosting of the website and its server functions.
  • Google Fonts: the Inter typeface is loaded from Google, which means your browser contacts Google servers (and shares your IP address) when a page loads.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own. We share information beyond them only if the law requires it.

5. Cookies and similar storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app stores a sign-in session in your browser, and, when it runs without an account, your leads in your browser’s local storage. The extension stores your pairing code and a few counters in the browser’s extension storage. This is needed for the app to work and stays on your device.

The website has hooks for privacy-friendly usage analytics, but they stay switched off unless you consent, and none is active today. If that changes we will update this page first.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your business description, leads and share links stay until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
  • Server logs are kept for a short period by our hosting provider.

7. Your choices and rights

You can remove any lead in the app at any time, disconnect the extension, turn off any share link, and download your leads as a CSV file. To get a copy of your information, correct it or delete your account, write to support@ownedmap.com from the email address on the account. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, to move your data elsewhere, and to complain to your local data protection authority. The same applies to the people who appear in leads, as described in section 2. We honor these requests where the law applies.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Each account can only read and change its own leads, and this is enforced in the database itself. No online service can promise perfect security, so please choose a strong password and protect your email account well.

9. Children

Owned Map is not meant for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and, when the change affects how we use your data, tell you in the app or by email.
