        Everything you need to know before you start.

        Owned Map is the workspace for everything you own. Put your property, vehicles, boats, aircraft, land, parking and equipment on one interactive map, and see the value, income, expenses and yield of each asset side by side, plus your net worth.

        Yes, Owned Map is live. Press Start for Free, begin your 7-day free trial on our payment page, choose a password and start adding your own assets right away. The dashboard on this page runs on sample data so you can try the map, filters and reports first.

        Houses and apartments, offices and commercial buildings, warehouses and industrial units, land, parking spaces, cars, trucks, motorcycles, motorhomes, yachts, sailboats, jet skis, aircraft and heavy equipment. For each one you can add the mortgage or loan on it, attach documents such as deeds and insurance, and set reminders for renewals.

        Press Start for Free. On our payment page you start a 7-day free trial (a card is required and is charged only when the trial ends, unless you cancel before). Then you choose a password and you are in. To add assets, upload a deed, invoice or contract and the details fill in by themselves, or import a CSV of what you already have. Next time, sign in with your email and password; if you forget it, use “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page.

        From you, so they always match what you believe an asset is worth. Enter each asset’s estimated value, purchase cost, yearly income, expenses and any debt, and Owned Map calculates appreciation, net income, yield and your net worth (value minus debt). If you own only a share of an asset, set your percentage and every figure counts just your part. It does not pull market prices. Each time you update a value, the change is added to that asset’s history and shown in the Performance view.

        Yes. The map has a Globe view and a Country view. Pick a country from the flags to fly there and see the assets you own in it. Place an asset in any city in the list, or add any place in the world with its coordinates.

        Yes, whenever you want. From the Reports tab you can download a PDF report, an Excel workbook (.xlsx) or a CSV file. You can also import assets from a CSV to start from a spreadsheet you already have.

        Your assets are saved to your own account, and each account can only read and change its own assets. The sample data on this page is never stored, and this page does not collect personal information. You can delete any asset at any time, and ask us to delete your account whenever you like.

        You start at $0: the 7-day free trial lets you try everything, and you are charged only when it ends. After that it is $47 per month, which covers 3 assets. Each additional asset adds $15 per month. Every feature is included in every plan, and you can cancel before the trial ends.

        Yes. In the app you can create a private read-only link for anyone you choose. They do not need an account and cannot change anything. You decide whether it includes debt, set an expiry date, and can turn the link off at any time. Notes, documents and reminders are never shared.
