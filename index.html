        FAQ

        Questions, answered.

        Everything you may want to know before you start.

        Owned Map is a visual workspace for everything you own. Put your property, vehicles, boats, aircraft, land, parking, and equipment on one interactive map, and see the value, income, expenses, and yield of each asset side by side.

        Owned Map is in early access. You can open the app, sign in with your email, and start adding your own assets. The dashboard on this page runs on illustrative sample data so you can try the map, filters, and reports before you sign in. Join the list to get launch updates.

        Houses and apartments, offices and commercial buildings, warehouses and industrial units, land, parking spaces, cars, trucks, motorcycles, motorhomes, yachts, sailboats, jet skis, aircraft, and heavy equipment. For each one you can add the mortgage or loan on it, attach documents such as deeds and insurance, and set reminders for renewals.

        During early access, accounts are opened by invitation. Join the early-access list above and we will send you your sign-in details; then you sign in with your email and password.

        From you. You enter each asset’s estimated value, purchase cost, yearly income and expenses, and any debt on it, and Owned Map calculates appreciation, net income, yield, and your net worth (value minus debt) from those figures. If you own only a share of an asset, set your percentage and every figure counts just your part. It does not pull market prices. Every time you update an estimated value, the change is added to that asset’s history and shown in the Performance view.

        Yes. The map has a Globe view and a Country view. Pick a country from the flags to fly there and see the assets you own in it. You can place an asset in any city in the list, or add any place in the world with its coordinates.

        Yes. From the Reports tab you can download a PDF report, an Excel workbook (.xlsx), or a CSV file. You can also import assets from a CSV to start from a spreadsheet you already have.

        Your assets are saved to your own account, and each account can only read and change its own assets. The sample data on this page is never stored. The only thing this website collects is your email address and your consent when you join the early-access list. You can delete any asset at any time, and ask us to remove your email whenever you like.

        Early-access pricing is $47 per month, which covers tracking 3 different assets. Each additional asset adds $15 per month. Every feature is included in every plan. You are not charged when you join the early-access list, and pricing may change before launch.

        You share your email and give consent. We only use it to contact you about Owned Map early access and launch updates, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

        Yes. In the app you can create a private read-only link for anyone you choose. They do not need an account and cannot change anything. You decide whether it includes debt, set an expiry date, and can turn the link off at any time. Notes, documents, and reminders are never shared.
