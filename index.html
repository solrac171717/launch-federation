        Everything you need to know before you start.

        Owned Map gets you customers from LinkedIn on autopilot. You paste your website, it works out your niche and the phrases buyers use on LinkedIn, and gives you the searches to open. As you browse, the Chrome extension scores each post, and the buyers appear on a global map with what they said and a reply you can copy.

        LinkedIn is the main source. The Owned Map extension for Chrome reads the posts and profiles you already have open, sends them to your account, and the AI keeps the ones that show buying intent. We give you the LinkedIn searches that fit your business, so every post you see is worth reading. Hacker News is added as a second source, with no extension needed. The extension is new and not yet in the Chrome Web Store, so you install it by hand (the steps are in the app). Reddit and X are not included.

        Finding, scoring, placing on the map and drafting the message happen without you doing anything. You approve the leads you want to contact, and the extension sends the invitation or message from your own browser and your own LinkedIn session, a few a day with pauses (you set the limit, 25 at most). Nothing goes to someone you did not queue. Each lead comes with a draft message and an invitation note that you edit. By default the extension fills them in and you press Send; you can switch on sending for you.

        A language model reads each post against what you sell and gives it 0 to 100: 80 or more means the person asks for it or for an alternative to a rival, 40 to 79 means they describe the problem, and anything lower is dropped. It is a guide, not a promise: always read the post.

        Posts rarely say where the person is. We place a lead only when the post or the author’s own profile text states a city. The others are listed in the Leads tab, and with the LinkedIn extension a lead is placed when you open their profile.

        Honestly: it is a risk, and you should know it. LinkedIn’s terms restrict automated tools, and LinkedIn can limit or close an account that sends too much. That is why everything runs in your own browser, we never ask for your LinkedIn password or keep your session, sending is capped per day with random pauses, and by default you press Send yourself. You are responsible for how you use your account.

        You start at $0: the 7-day free trial lets you try everything, and you are charged only when it ends. After that it is $47 per month, which includes 75 leads with a score of 70 or more. Each additional pack of 25 leads adds $15 per month for packs 4 to 10, then $10 for packs 11 to 25 and $5 from the 26th, up to 100 packs (2,500 leads). Pay yearly and save 30%.

        Your leads and your business description are saved in your own account, and each account can only read and change its own rows. You can remove any lead, or all the sample leads, in one click, and ask us to delete your account whenever you like.
