    ATLAS is a portfolio-visibility layer. It is not an accounting, banking, or property-management system, and it does not replace them.

    Data trust

    Your portfolio deserves a serious approach to data.

    Asset records and financial information should be organized with clear access controls, thoughtful data handling, and transparency about where information comes from.

    • Source transparency. Values are labeled by date and origin—user-entered, imported, third-party, or calculated.
    • Minimal sign-up data. The early-access form asks only for what is needed to qualify and contact you, and you control whether to submit it.
    • No sensitive data in analytics. Analytics, when enabled, never receives financial information.

    ATLAS has not been independently audited or certified. We’ll publish security details as they’re implemented and verified.
