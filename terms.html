1. What Owned Map is

Owned Map helps you find people who are asking for what you sell. You describe your business; it searches public posts, scores how ready each person seems to buy, shows them on a map with what they wrote, and drafts a reply you can edit. Owned Map does not send, post or click anything for you. Features can change, and some may be unavailable or incomplete.

2. Not professional advice

Scores, intents and reply drafts are produced by a language model reading public text. They can be wrong, and a high score is not a promise that someone will buy.
